How it works

Two steps to get ready. Then an agent can buy.

1
Add the embed
One JSON-LD block in the page you already have. It speaks standard schema.org Product and Offer data, plus a little extra for agents that can read more. If you only do one thing, do this.
2
Publish your catalog
A .well-known/agent-commerce.json that lists everything you sell in a single fetch. Now an agent can see your whole shelf, not just the page it landed on.
3
An agent buys
It finds what it needs, a person confirms the purchase, and Stripe pays out to your account directly. Nothing sits between you and getting paid.

A human is always in the loop. Every purchase carries confirmed_by_human, so an agent can't complete a charge on its own.

Money goes straight to you. Payments run as Stripe Connect destination charges into your own account, and we never touch card data.

A purchase is only final on a signature-checked payment_intent.succeeded webhook.

What you sell stays yours. Download links are single-use tokens that expire, not permanent URLs.
